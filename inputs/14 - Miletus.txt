@Chapter
title=Miletus

&date AD 56 Miletus
&date 23 years after the crucifixion

``Greetings, oh faithful of Ephesus, whose names are
written in the book of life and will be saved.  And
thank you all for making the journey here today to
see me for what I earnestly feel will be the last
time that we will see each other face to face.

``You know how, not so long ago, I lived among you
in Ephesus.  And you will certainly remember the
troubles that led me to leave you a year ago.

``And you are doubtless aware that these troubles
were not unique to Ephesus, but have dogged me
everywhere since I left Antioch.  They have accused
me of many things; things of which you can be my
witnesses I am not guilty.

``They have accused me of overweening pride; but you
know that since I first arrived in Asia, I have
always humbled myself before the cause of serving
the Lord.

``They have accused me of falsehoods; but you know
that I have never shrunk from telling the truth,
even when it was unpalatable and difficult even
for you to hear.

``They have accused me of denying God; but you know
that I have never done anything but urged repentance
of sins on all men, be they Jew or Gentile.

``They have accused me of violating the Law; but you
know that while I deny the necessity of strict
observance of the Law for justification, I have never
myself transgressed so much as one of the provisions
of the Law.

``They have accused me of covetousness and a
desire for self-enrichment; but you can attest
that in all the time I was with you I took nothing
for my own gain that I had not earned.  And that
this collection which you have raised and brought
to me here today is not for my own benefit,
but for the benefit of the poor, needy and hungry
in Judea.

``They have accused me of living my life without
respect to the demands of God; but you today will
witness with what heaviness I take on the next task
that God has laid before me.

``For, brothers, the Spirit compels me to go to
Jerusalem, as it compelled Jesus before me.  And
as he was betrayed and died there, I know that I
too will be betrayed and sent to be killed there.

``But I also know, brothers, that in the same way
that their oh-so-cunning human trap misfired and
redounded to the greater glory of God, that their
traps will rebound on them again and God will be
the victor.  Because you know that God always
gives us the victory through our faith in his
son Christ Jesus.

``Indeed, indeed, it is a fair question.  Does the
Holy Spirit tell me that my life is in danger, or
is it just practical experience?  A fair question
indeed.

``Well, let us review the evidence.  I have been
beaten and left for dead; I have been chased out of cities;
I have been arrested and hauled in front of magistrates
more times than I care to remember, including with
you in Ephesus.

``What then does this tell us about the nature of our
foes?  That they are persistent; that they continue
to ignore the clear signs of the times; that we can
only continue to pray for their repentance and
deliverance.

``And I do, honestly, know from the Spirit that not one
of you among whom I have moved as I preached the kingdom
of God will ever see my face again in Asia.

``But my conscience is clear:  I have taught you
well, and you yourselves have proved out the faith which
I had in you, and God has delivered hundredfold fruit
in you.

``And I know now that as I move on from this theatre
to others - whether to live or to die - that you are
no longer the sheep needing to be fed, watered, folded
and carded, but are the shepherds, ready to gather
your own flocks and you are now the guardians of the
brotherhood in Asia, even at the cost of your own blood.

``For it will not be an easy ride for you, brothers 
and sisters!  Oh, no!  Never think that because I leave
you all the strife and discord will suddenly go away, as
if I were the cause of it!

``But no! The cause of the discord is as it ever has
been; the root cause of all evil is the devil at
work abroad in the world.  While I am here, I am
the figurehead and they will do everything they can
to unseat me.  But when I am gone, to be betrayed
and slaughtered elsewhere, then they will turn
their attentions on you, my brothers.

``Like ravening wolves they will come among you, taking
you for sheep.  And if sheep, they will trap you and
kill you and the word of God, God's glorious word of
repentance and hope given unto us through the death and
resurrection of his son, and even more so by the
life and teaching of the man Christ Jesus, this teaching
I say, they will try to blot out from the earth.

``But you are not sheep!  Not you!  No, you are the
shepherds of Asia, ready to stand against the evil one.
And I know that you will protect each others and you
will protect those among the nations who are still sheep
and need your strength.

``And you will be strong, brothers!  You will be strong for
all those who need you to be strong.

``But when the direct attacks fail, they will try more
subtle means to subvert they faith.  They will come among
you and tell you that they are also followers of the Way.
They will come among you and tell you that they are your
brothers.  Lies!  Heinous Lies!

``But how will you tell?  For surely you have other
brothers, and they will, from time to time come among
you, and they will tell you the truth - that they are
your brothers.  And the lies will sound like the truth
and the truth will sound like the lies.

``But the Spirit knows, my brothers.  The Spirit can
always distinguish the Truth from the Lie.  And if
the Spirit of Truth is in you, then you will know the
Truth, and you will see the lie and you will be strong
in those days!
 
``While I was with you for all those months, I never
failed to take an opportunity to make sure you were
instructed and grounded in the faith.  And I ensured
that you were baptised and had received the Holy
Spirit.  And now you have received the Holy Spirit,
it will tell you what is true and what is not true.

``Remember that you have repented!  Remember always
that you are saved and that your names are written
in the book of Life!  The end times are now, my
brothers, and like Christ before me, I go to Jerusalem
thinking that this could be the one event that brings
about the final Day of Judgment.

``And where will you be on that Day, brothers?

``This, more than anything else, is the one question
which I want you to ask yourselves and to ask each
other daily: where will I be on Judgment Day?

``Where will you be?  You have been saved.  You have
believed now in the revelation of Christ Jesus.  You
have taken the baptism of the faith.  You have taken
the first steps on the road to complete freedom.  And
there is no going back.

``And thus, in parting with you all for the last
time, I commend you to the care of the Holy Spirit,
in whose hands are the outcomes of life.  In His
hands you are secure.  In the knowledge and love
of Christ Jesus you are saved.
