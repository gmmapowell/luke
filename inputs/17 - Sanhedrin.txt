@Chapter
title=Sanhedrin

&date AD56 Jerusalem
&date 23 years after the crucifixion

``Men and brothers, I have lived my life with a perfectly clear conscience
before God up to the present day.  I keep faith with God's Law as revealed
by Moses, and I abide by the words of the prophets.  I praise God with the
psalms, and I revere the unique history that has been gifted to the Jewish
people.  And thus, my brothers, have I lived my entire life.

``It is not to be supposed that because I have gone and preached the salvation
of the new revelation I have received through Jesus Christ throughout Asia and
Greece to Gentiles as well as Jews that I reject our heritage; nor is it
reasonable to say that I would profane the Temple or the customs of our people.

``Of what, then, am I accused?  Of trumped-up falsehoods?  You know as well as
I do that I did _not_ bring any Gentiles into the Holy Spaces of the Temple.
God forbid that I should do such a thing.

``Am I perhaps accused of spreading God's word to the Gentiles?  Is that
a crime?  To spread the word of God to those who have not had the opportunity
to hear it previously?

``Or is it possibly the crime of saying that these people are now welcome
in the province of our God and can be saved?

``Once there was a man named Abraham.  Before God promised to make nations
out of his children, he was just a man, uncircumised as the Gentiles.  But
God found him through his faith - faith by which he came out of the land of
his birth and his idols.  And after God found him, he led him to the land
which was promised him - and then has been promised to us as the children
of his inheritance.

``How, now, then are the Gentiles of today in a different position to
Abraham centuries ago?  They, like he, know nothing of God.  Like he,
they are uncircumised.  But what if they, like he, are prepared to emerge
from the cities and leave their idols and their superstitions and come home
to have faith in the one true God?

``You may say it is a sweetheart deal!  But if the Law chafes on you so strongly,
ask yourselves why you think that is.  Was man made for the Law or the Law for
the man?  Maybe you should consider giving up your hope of salvation and apostasising
as you accuse me of doing?

``Of course it is hard to obey the Law!  If it were so easy everybody did it
all the time, it wouldn't be `the Law' but just natural habits.

``And it was because of how hard it is to follow the Law that God sent Jesus
to call his lost sheep to repentance and a deeper understanding of the Law.
Remember the words of the prophet Jeremiah:

``And the day will one day come when I will cease this stupid repetition `obey
the Law' for in that day I will write the Law into their very bones, and then
they will all be my children, and then it will truly be the case that I will
be the God that they worship.

``But in the days of Jeremiah that was not the case and God had to punish them
again and again because they drifted away from his words again and again.
And today?  How is it any different today?  Your very argument that it is
hard to obey the Law tells against you!  It is not written on your inside;
you simply obey it from the outside, as an imposition of a cruel and unjust
God!

``Arrgh!  For striking me, God will strike you, you empty-headed idiot!

``So what did God do?  He sent Christ Jesus to show you the effortless
nature of obeying the will of God, and to pour out on all willing to receive
it his Holy Spirit, that should infuse them and carry out his promise to write
the Law on their insides.

``And how did you respond?  By crucifying him!  The very man that had
been sent to call sinners to repentance, you murdered as a sinner himself!

``No, no I cannot claim to be truly innocent.  No, I was not hear when Jesus
himself was crucified.  No, I applauded the actions taken against his followers
in the early days.  I applauded when Stephen was taken and stoned.  I, myself,
prosecuted, argued and disputed with followers of this Way across Syria from
Antioch to Damascus.

``And then the miracle occurred.  Jesus himself appeared to me as to one born
out of the due course of nature.  And his Spirit fell upon me, and baptised me
and purified me of my self-belief and sin that was blinding me.  And in
humility, I accepted the words of God and repented of my sins and changed my
ways.

``How then could I help myself but to preach this to others, still living in
the blindness from which I had escaped?  How could I hold fast to myself the
blessings I had received while others languished in the darkness?

``Tell me, how!

``And yes, I preached that Jesus died for us and that Jesus rose for us, because
I know first-hand that he did!  Yes, I do!  Because he appeared unto me, and how
could that be if he were not risen from the dead!

``Indeed that is true, you Sadducees deny the resurrection in all its forms.
But I am a Pharisee, a son of Pharisees and of generations of Pharisees, and
I say unto you that the resurrection is real, the bodily resurrection is real
and all will be resurrected in the final day for judgment, and _that day will
not long be delayed!

``And I perceive that in truth, it is this for which I am on trial!  It is
for my hope in the resurrection of the dead and my preaching of it!''
