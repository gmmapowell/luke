28. Defence

AD58 Caesarea

25 years after the resurrection

``Exalted Governor, Lucius Porcius Festus, your highness, King Agrippa, and your Ladyship, Queen Berenice. I am grateful for the opportunity you have given me to present my case in the only court competent to consider the fates of members of the Roman Empire.

``It is well known that I am a Citizen of Tarsus in Asia. It is well known to all the Jews there that I am a Pharisee of no mean standing, and that my father and his father were Jews, Citizens and Pharisees!

``It is also well known that from my youth I have known the Law, recited the Law, studied the Law, taught the Law, practiced the Law. Nobody can truthfully bring against me a charge of acting contrary to the Law. As for this charge today, I say it is not truthful. It is a total fabrication.

``Why then am I on trial, if it is not for defiling the Temple by bringing Gentiles into it? Very simply, it is for socializing with Gentiles at all! It is well known also that Jews believe themselves to be superior to other races - including Romans! - because they are descended from Abraham, with whom God entered into direct dialogue and promised this land to his seed for ever.

``And descent from Abraham then becomes a gift most precious! Only through descent from Abraham can any man be admitted to the coming Kingdom of Heaven! And in order to qualify, a man must be without sin. And what is the definition of sin? Well, you need to know the law and obey all its commandments. And they are many. Some, like Hillel, manage to reduce it to two - continually love God and always love your fellow man - many offer a ten-commandment summary from the Torah as summarizing the Law, but the truth is that there are over five hundred commands in the Law, and to be free of sin a man must obey all of them for his entire life!

``Is this even possible, I ask you? Some of these commandments seem from time to time to conflict with each other, and doctors of the Law can spend weeks or months arguing about the minutiae of the Law.

``To take but one example: the Sabbath is a day of rest. I am sure that you are well aware of this, even in your short time here. But where does rest end and indolence begin? There are lawyers who discuss the exact number of steps you can take on the Sabbath. Cooking is work, and so cooking is not allowed on the Sabbath; on the other hand, eating is allowed, provided the food was prepared the day before.

``Some consider looking after children to be work - which should not be done on the Sabbath - but how can it be avoided? The children must be fed and clothed - they cannot do this for themselves.

``We are commanded to honour our parents - but what if our parents deviate from the Law? Should we still honour them? Should we honour their deviance from the Law? Or, should we take them to task for their sins, which is also a requirement of the Law?

``So many lived under the Law, and many died under the Law. But what happens after death? Some say that once dead, there is nothing more. Some say that after death we will all be recalled in the Spirit. And some say we will be resurrected with our bodies restored and transformed.

``These men that you see before you who accuse me of defiling the Temple are Sadducees. They do not believe that there is any resurrection of the dead. As a Pharisee, I believe in the bodily resurrection and the final judgment. And it is for this they hound me and pursue me. If you find me guilty today, you do not find me guilty of the crime of defiling the Temple - they know as well as I do that I did not do that - but you find that I am wrong about the resurrection. But that is not a criminal matter.

``Why then, do they pursue me with these deadly charges, and plot against me to kill me? Because I preach the Truth, which is that one has already been raised from the dead, and this denies everything that they believe: how can there be no resurrection if one has already been raised from the dead?

``The life of Jesus, called Christ, is a matter of public record. His death is also a matter of public record, since it was in the time of the Governor Pontius Pilate that he was crucified. He was crucified after the plotting of the leaders of the Jews. Why did they plot against him? Because he taught that the Law was to be superseded by a new covenant between God and men to replace the covenant agreed with Abraham. In this new covenant, all men could participate - not just the Jews. But first, he insisted, it was important to preach the word to all the lost sheep of Israel: the descendants of Abraham who could not follow the Law because its demands were too onerous.

``And so God sent Jesus to preach a new Way forward: that the new qualification for the resurrection was to literally follow the formula of Hillel: in all things to love God and follow his Way; and to live this in daily life by loving all men.

``If you see those who follow the Way in Judea - or indeed, those I have converted in Asia and Greece - they live in communities together, not competitively but collaboratively. They earn what they can individually, but they share it among all their neighbours.

``This man came and preached that salvation was within reach of all - it was not a hard obligation which only the rich and indolent could achieve, but a simple matter of living simply and sharing God's bounty with neighbours - and he overturned the established order. He said that those with power and influence were not superior to any others in the eyes of God. And he made friends with beggars, thieves and prostitutes and shunned the company of the rich and the priests.

``And so they plotted to disgrace him and, when that failed, they plotted to kill him. They tried many things to trick him and sent many of the clever doctors of the Law to ask him difficult questions, but always he evaded their traps and gave answers that were wise beyond consideration.

``I was not one of the plotters. But that was simply because I was not in Jerusalem but in Antioch. I heard of these things at first vaguely and from afar. It was probably a year or two before the first of the followers of the Way came to Antioch and preached the obsolescence of the Law. I was outraged! The Law, the very pathway to holiness and eternal life, obsolete? What hope did I have of the resurrection if the Law had been outdated? And by such as flimsy construction! `Love your neighbour'. As if we did not do this every day.

``You see, without meeting the man, without hearing his teaching, without the deeper sense of the way in which he instructed his followers to `love their neighbour' the command seemed empty and hollow.

``And so, I opposed these men. In the synagogues I disputed with them; I argued with them; I showed them irrefutable proofs of the sole validity of the Law! Yes, I argued all the same things that my prosecutors argue today to show that I am in the wrong.

``But the trump card is the bodily resurrection of Jesus. When I first heard this advanced, I thought the men who argued it were either desperate, lying or mad. Or all three. That God should raise a man from the dead - ludicrous! That he should do it without the kind of fanfare that would make everybody pay attention - even more ludicrous! I called them out in their preaching for their madness.

``And after I had done away with them in Antioch - by fair means or foul - I petitioned the leaders of the synagogues in Antioch that I should be sent to Damascus, because some of these followers of the Jesus Cult had fled there from the persecutions in Jerusalem and Antioch. And they agreed and sent me.

``The leader of the Way in Damascus was, I had heard, a man named Ananias. And I went specifically to argue with him and, if all else failed, to arrest him on charges of blasphemy - that the Law had passed away; much the same charge with which I am accused today.

``But I never made it to Damascus. At least, the person that I was then never made it. When I was nearly there, there was suddenly a bright light in the sky and I was blinded. And a voice called out to me `Why, Paul, why? Why do you try to persecute me? The harder you push, the harder it will be for you?'

``And I was stunned and I could not fathom what was happening so I called out, `Who, Lord? Who am I persecuting? And how? What shall I do?'

``And he said, `you know you are persecuting, Paul. I am Jesus, from Nazareth. Now stand up and walk, for I have chosen you as a special messenger - you are to take my message to the Gentiles and the lands of the Gentiles and you are to preach to them in Asia and Greece and Rome!'

``But when I stood again, I found I could not see. I had been blinded by the bright light and had to be led by the hand of my companions. And when I came to Damascus, the very man whom I had gone to persecute - Ananias - came and found me, and healed me of my blindness!

``Thus, since that time, I have not been able to sit still but have travelled across Asia and Greece preaching what I myself have learned - that Jesus is God's Christ, that he was unjustly killed by the leaders of the Jews in collaboration with the Roman governor, that he rose again from the dead, and that by accepting his death as a token for the remission of our own sins, we are freed from the Law and can partake in the bodily resurrection ourselves! We are free, I say!''
