Preface

AD 50 Troas

17 years since the Crucifixion of Jesus

``Have you heard the news? The Good News that comes from God?

``Let me tell you, for I promise you that the time of fulfillment of the promise is now. The time has come and the promises are being kept.

``Jesus, a man, but truly the Son of God, who was born of a woman, as a human descended from the ancient Jewish King David and marked out as the Son of God by the Spirit of Holiness which enabled him to conquer all the ills of this world - even his own degrading death.

``All this, I tell you, was foreseen by the prophets of old time and is to be found promised by them in the Holy Scriptures.

``It is from this man, I say, that we have received the grace of God and are now saved - are indeed, in our own way, the children of God through adoption even as Jesus was his true Son.

``Who was then this Jesus?

``God sent many prophets to point the way of repentance to the people of Israel. Sometimes they listened, and often they did not. They came, they prophesied and those who listened repented of their sins.

``Thirty years ago, God sent another prophet, John by name, he who was foreseen by Isaiah: `The voice of one crying in the wilderness.' In the wilderness he preached; in the wilderness he baptised; and in the wilderness many repented of their sins.

``And many discussed among themselves whether this might be the Chosen One - God's son. But when they put the question to John he said `no, it is not I. But the time will be soon. Repent and hear the Gospel.'

``But one day came a man to John for baptism that John knew, for it was his cousin. And he denied him baptism, for, being a holy man of God, he knew that this was God's son, the one who, in his own words, would baptise others, `only not with water, but with the blazing fires of Heaven.

``But Jesus in humility insisted on baptism, that all the forms might be observed: that he might be truly ready to begin his own ministry.

``And what a ministry it was! Unlike others, he did not need to argue long details of the law, but he told the truth, the truth that came directly from God.

``Jesus did not come, as many thought he would, as a King, born into a Palace, with a royal robe and a diadem, but lived humbly, his early years spent as a journeyman carpenter, giving quality work for a fair price and treating no man unfairly or dishonestly.

``And he did not come to reinforce the prejudices of this world, to reward the rich and impoverish the poor. Listen to these sayings of Jesus:

`` `How happy are you who have nothing, for the Kingdom of God is open to you!

`` `How happy are you who feel hungry, for you will have plenty of nourishment!

`` `How happy are you who weep now, for you are going to laugh!'

``But Jesus did not come to teach, amazing as his words were. He came to change the course of human history.

``In proof of that, everywhere he went, people were transformed! Not just in their hearts, but in the bodies, too.

``Consider just two simple examples:

``One day when he was in Capernaum, a town in Galilee, he was teaching on the Sabbath day. And, as always, those who heard him were astonished, for his words had the ring of authority.

``There was a man in the synagogue under the influence of some evil spirit and he yelled at the top of his voice, "Hi! What have you got to do with us, Jesus, you Nazarene - have you come to kill us? I know who you are all right, you're God's holy one!"

``Jesus cut him short and spoke sharply, "Be quiet! Get out of him!" And after throwing the man down in front of them, the devil did come out of him without hurting him in the slightest. At this everybody present was amazed and they kept saying to each other, "What sort of words are these? He speaks to these evil spirits with authority and power and out they come."

``When he was in one of the towns of Galilee, he came upon a man who was a mass of leprosy. When he saw Jesus, he prostrated himself before him and begged, `If you want to, Lord, you can make me clean.'

``Jesus stretched out his hand, placed it on the leper, saying, `Certainly I want to. Be clean!'

``Immediately the leprosy left him and Jesus told him to go and show himself to the priest and to make the offerings for his recovery that Moses had prescribed, as evidence to the authorities.

``So, you might imagine that all the people repented of their wrongdoing and all was harmony.

``No!

``That day is coming; indeed, that day will very shortly be upon us. Christ will return in his glory, shown at the right hand of God himself and all will be judged! Woe to them who are found wanting when that day comes, for it will not be pleasant to have not repented on that day! They will be whittled from the wheat like chaff and only the righteous will remain.

``But no, those who felt threatened by the Truth, by the Son of God incarnate among them and telling it as it really is, they gathered together and tried to entrap him, and make a fool of him in front of the people, and discredit him.

``Ha! But they couldn't, you know, they couldn't. They tried and they tried, but they were mere men against the word of God. And the devices of men cannot stand against the word of God.

``Consider this:

``One of the Jewish rulers put this question to him, `Master, I know that you are good; tell me, please, what must I do to be sure of eternal life?'

`` `I wonder why you call me good?' returned Jesus. 'No person is good - only the one God. But surely you know the commandments - 'Do not commit adultery,' 'Do not murder,' 'Do not steal,' 'Do not bear false witness,' 'Honour your father and your mother'. Are these not enough to guarantee eternal life? '

`` `All these,' he replied, `I have carefully kept since I was quite young.'

``So when Jesus heard that, he said to him, `In that case, I see that there is still one thing you have missed. Sell everything you possess and give the money away to the poor, and you will have riches in Heaven.

`` `Then come and follow me.'

``But when he heard this, he was greatly distressed for he was very rich.

``And Jesus, when he saw how his face fell, remarked, `How difficult it is for those who have great possessions to enter the kingdom of God! A camel loaded down with goods could squeeze through the gate of a city more easily than a man weighed down with his riches could get into the kingdom of God.'

``So what did the dark plotters do? Did they see the error of their ways and repent? Hardly!

``Now the holy anger of God is disclosed from Heaven against the godlessness and evil of those men who render truth dumb and inoperative by their wickedness. It is not that they do not know the truth about God; indeed he has made it quite plain to them. For since the beginning of the world the invisible attributes of God have been plainly discernible through things which he has made and which are commonly seen and known, thus leaving these men without a rag of excuse. They knew all the time that there is a God, yet they refused to acknowledge him as such, or to thank him for what he is or does. Thus they became fatuous in their argumentations, and plunged their silly minds still further into the dark.

``Behind a facade of "wisdom" they became just fools, fools who would exchange the glory of the eternal God for an imitation image of a mortal man, or of creatures that run or fly or crawl.

``And they found one man, a disciple of Jesus', who they could corrupt into laying false charges against him and betraying him into the hands of the rulers of the Jews.

``And they did it late at night, when the crowds had gone home. Under cover of darkness, in the devil's hour, they did their work.

``Yet we cannot judge them, for we also are sinners: God is the only judge

``Now if you feel inclined to set yourself up as a judge of those who sin, let me assure you, whoever you are, that you are in no position to do so. For at whatever point you condemn others you automatically condemn yourself, since you, the judge, commit the same sins. God's judgment, we know, is utterly impartial in its action against such evil-doers. What makes you think that you who so readily judge the sins of others, can consider yourself beyond the judgment of God?

``Are you by your obstinate refusal to repent simply storing up for yourself an experience of the wrath of God in the day when, in his holy anger against evil, he shows his hand in righteous judgment?

``So they took him privately and handed him over to the Roman governor on charges of sedition and corruption.

``But even if this they failed - for the Roman governor himself could find nothing wrong with him! I ask you, would you be confident that the Governor of Asia would find nothing wrong with you? Indeed; their venality is a by-word. And yet, and yet, no fault at all could he find in Jesus.

``But they were not to be denied, these who so value their `faith' that they would deny the very Son of God! No, no! Faced with the refusal even of the Roman Governor to put an innocent man to death, they begged, pleaded and cajoled, even threatened to riot until they got their way.

``So the man Jesus was killed by the Romans, crucified like a common criminal. And the wicked ones were satisfied.

``But wait! God could not abide that death should be shown to be more powerful than His Will! By a mighty act, God did restore his son from the grave, as he has promised all will be raised at the end of days.

``He did not raise up a corruptible body, corruptible as the human body before death, subject to sin, suffering and death, but an incorruptible body, a body of light and glory.

``Yes, yes - a human body, to be sure - he was recognized by his followers and those to whom he appeared. But a body transformed through the resurrection! And yet, there are still those who say, `but there is no resurrection.'

``But let us be very clear on this point. God, by performing the resurrection of his son Jesus, did very clearly show that the resurrection is real. It is no longer a hypothetical. It is happening now.

``And I say to you that the ministry and resurrection of Jesus is just the first of fruits of the tree of the Spirit. The Messianic age is dawning. Before long, my brethren, Jesus will return in all his power and glory and will divide the chaff from the wheat. There will be great celebration in that day - and also great mourning. Who will you be in that day?

``Consider this parable which Jesus spoke before he was crucified:

``You must be ready dressed and have your lamps alight, like men who wait to welcome their lord and master on his return from the wedding-feast, so that when he comes and knocks at the door, they may open it for him at once. Happy are the servants whom their lord finds on the alert when he arrives. I assure you that he will take off his outer clothes, make then sit down to dinner, and come and wait on them. And if he should come just after midnight or in the very early morning, and find them still on the alert, their happiness is assured. But be certain of this, that if the householder had known the time when the burglar would come, he would not have let his house be broken into. So you must be on the alert, for the Son of Man is coming at a time when you may not expect him.

``You ask for proof? What further proof do you need than that I stand here? For I am a man myself who has seen these things with my own eyes! Jesus, being crucified and then restored to life by the grace of God, appeared to me, in person, in his restored flesh and showed me the wrong I was doing in persecuting his disciples in Antioch!

``The presence of the Holy Spirit in those who follow our Way is all the proof anyone could need? And yet, and yet you ask for more proof. Can there be certain knowledge for such as you, you doubters?

``But listen and hear and be converted, you wicked ones!

``Right now we are seeing the righteousness of God declared quite apart from the Law: it is a righteousness imparted to, and operating in, all who have faith in Jesus Christ. There is no longer going to be any distinction to be made anywhere: everyone has sinned, everyone falls short of the beauty of God's plan. Anyone who has faith is now freely acquitted in the eyes of God by his generous dealing in the redemptive act of Jesus Christ. God has appointed him as the means of propitiation, a propitiation accomplished by the shedding of his blood, to be received and made effective in ourselves by faith.

``Are we then undermining the Law by this insistence on faith? Not a bit of it! We put the Law in its proper place.

``Now if a man works his wages are not counted as a gift but as a fair reward. But if a man, irrespective of his work, has faith as righteousness, then that man's faith is counted as righteousness, and that is the gift of God. This is the happy state of the man whom God accounts righteous, apart from his achievements, as David expresses it: 'Blessed are those whose lawless deeds are forgiven, and whose sins are covered; blessed is the man to whom the Lord shall not impute sin'.

``Since then it is by faith that we are justified, let us grasp the fact that we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through him we have confidently entered into this new relationship of grace, and here we take our stand, in happy certainty of the glorious things he has for us in the future.

``This doesn't mean, of course, that we have only a hope of future joys - we can be full of joy here and now even in our trials and troubles. Taken in the right spirit these very things will give us patient endurance; this in turn will develop a mature character, and a character of this sort produces a steady hope, a hope that will never disappoint us. Already we have some experience of the love of God flooding through our hearts by the Holy Spirit given to us.

``And we can see that while we were powerless to help ourselves, Christ died for sinful men. In human experience it is a rare thing for one man to give his life for another, even if the latter be a good man, though there have been a few who have had the courage to do it. Yet the proof of God's amazing love is this: that it was while we were sinners that Christ died for us.

``Moreover, if he did that for us while we were sinners, now that we are men justified by the shedding of his blood, what reason have we to fear the wrath of God? If, while we were his enemies, Christ reconciled us to God by dying for us, surely now that we are reconciled we may be perfectly certain of our salvation through his living in us. Nor, I am sure, is this a matter of bare salvation - we may hold our heads high in the light of God's love because of the reconciliation which Christ has made.

``Now, what is the Law of Moses, except a guide to avoid sin? Yet, though sin is shown to be wide and deep, thank God his grace is wider and deeper still! Sin used to be the master of men and in the end handed them over to death: now grace is the ruling factor, with righteousness as its purpose and its end the bringing of men to the eternal life of God through Jesus Christ our Lord.

``So, what do we do now? Shall we sin to our heart's content and see how far we can exploit the grace of God? What a ghastly thought! We, who have died to sin - how could we live in sin a moment longer?

``No, the act of baptism is a sign of purification - as bathing washes away the dirt and grime of the day or of travel, so the act of baptism washes away the sins of the world. Having washed them away, would you really allow them back in?

``Do not, then, allow sin to establish any power over your mortal bodies in making you give way to your lusts. Nor hand over your organs to be, as it were, weapons of evil for the devil's purposes. But, like men rescued from certain death, put yourselves in God's hands as weapons of good for his own purposes. For sin is not meant to be your master - you are no longer living under the Law, but under grace.

``Thus, no condemnation now hangs over the head of those who are "in" Jesus Christ. For the new spiritual principle of life "in" Christ lifts me out of the old vicious circle of sin and death.

``But you are not so trapped if the Spirit of God finds a home within you. You cannot, indeed, be a Christian at all unless you have something of his Spirit in you. Now if Christ does live within you his presence means that your sinful nature is dead, but your spirit becomes alive because of the righteousness he brings with him. I said that our nature is "dead" in the presence of Christ, and so it is, because of its sin. Nevertheless once the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead lives within you he will, by that same Spirit, bring to your whole being new strength and vitality.

``All who follow the leading of God's Spirit are God's own sons. Nor are you meant to relapse into the old slavish attitude of fear - you have been adopted into the very family circle of God and you can say with a full heart, "Father, my Father". The Spirit himself endorses our inward conviction that we really are the children of God. Think what that means. If we are his children we share his treasures, and all that Christ claims as his will belong to all of us as well! Yes, if we share in his suffering we shall certainly share in his glory.

``The Spirit of God not only maintains this hope within us, but helps us in our present limitations. For example, we do not know how to pray worthily as sons of God, but his Spirit within us is actually praying for us in those agonising longings which never find words. And God who knows the heart's secrets understands, of course, the Spirit's intention as he prays for those who love God.

``Moreover we know that to those who love God, who are called according to his plan, everything that happens fits into a pattern for good. God, in his foreknowledge, chose them to bear the family likeness of his Son, that he might be the eldest of a family of many brothers. He chose them long ago; when the time came he called them, he made them righteous in his sight, and then lifted them to the splendour of life as his own sons.

``In face of all this, what is there left to say? If God is for us, who can be against us? He that did not hesitate to spare his own Son but gave him up for us all - can we not trust such a God to give us, with him, everything else that we can need?

``Who would dare to accuse us, whom God has chosen? The judge himself has declared us free from sin. Who is in a position to condemn? Only Christ, and Christ died for us, Christ rose for us, Christ reigns in power for us, Christ prays for us!

``Can anything separate us from the love of Christ? Can trouble, pain or persecution? Can lack of clothes and food, danger to life and limb, the threat of force of arms? Indeed some of us know the truth of the ancient text: 'For your sake we are killed all day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter'.

``No, in all these things we win an overwhelming victory through him who has proved his love for us.

``I have become absolutely convinced that neither death nor life, neither messenger of Heaven nor monarch of earth, neither what happens today nor what may happen tomorrow, neither a power from on high nor a power from below, nor anything else in God's whole world has any power to separate us from the love of God in Jesus Christ our Lord!

``This, then, is the secret of faith; this is exactly what we are preaching, and it says, "If you openly admit by your own mouth that Jesus Christ is the Lord, and if you believe in your own heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved." For it is believing in the heart that makes a man righteous before God, and it is stating his belief by his own mouth that confirms his salvation. And the scripture says: 'Whoever believes on him will not be put to shame'.

``And when I say "whoever", I mean anyone, without distinction between Jew or Greek, free or slave, citien or non-citizen. For all have the same Lord, whose boundless resources are available to all who turn to him in faith. For: 'Whoever calls upon the name of the Lord shall be saved'.

``Are you with me? Will you be saved? Will you call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ?''
