4. Kerygma

AD 50 Philippi

17 years after the crucifixion

``The coming of the Kingdom of God.

``The dream of the ages. All men will repent and we will come home to God. It has been preached and taught by all the prophets.

``Jeremiah said, `And I will write my law in their inward parts and they shall all know me, from the least unto the greatest.'

``But do you? In your inward parts? Do you feel the burning fire of the Holy Spirit as a daily force in your life?

``I am here today to show you that what has previously been nothing more than prophecy and promise is today fully realized in the life, death and resurrection of the promised Messiah.

``Yes, you may gasp and be shocked. You may even cry `blasphemy', but answer me this: Can it really be blasphemy to say that the promises are true? If not now, then when? Can we say that the endtimes have been promised but it is blasphemy to say that they have come? How does that make sense?

``And yet, if the time has come, and the promises are to be fulfilled, who are we to try and dispute that? Who are we to try and oppose the will of God?

``Wait, wait and I will get there. We have all day, good people of Philippi. Surely the news that tells of the arrival of the endtimes is worth giving over a few hours to? Nothing will ever be the same again!

``We have heard inspiring readings this morning. Readings that tell of sin, exile and restoration. Let me tell you more of this: stories of sin cured, of an unwonted death, and of a resurrection! And then see if you do not agree that the Messianic age has dawned.

``There was a preacher, sent from God named John. He went into the whole country round about the Jordan proclaiming baptism as a mark of a complete change of heart and of the forgiveness of sins, as we just heard the prophet Isaiah say - 'The voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make his paths straight. Every valley shall be filled, and every mountain and hill brought low; and the crooked places shall be made straight, and the rough ways made smooth; and all flesh shall see the salvation of God'

``And great crowds would come out to be baptised by him, and he would see them and question them as to their integrity. `Who warned you, you serpent's brood, to escape from the wrath to come? See that you do something to show that your hearts are really changed! Don't start thinking that you can say to yourselves, 'We are Abraham's children', for I tell you that God could produce children of Abraham out of mere stones! The axe already lies at the root of the tree, and the tree that fails to produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.'

``And he preached and prophesied by the River Jordan as did the prophets of old. And some who came truly repented of what they had done, and desisted from their habits, and were baptised and followed a new path.

``And others went away, thinking in their hearts that this was a madman who had fooled even himself into believing that he was a special one.

``And some asked whether he was, in fact, the promised one, the Messiah, the Christ, who should come to purge Israel of her sins and found the Kingdom of God. `No,' he declared, `that is not I; that is one greater than I; but before the day I die, he will be revealed unto you in his glory.'

``And one day came Jesus, a carpenter of Nazareth, to John and asked for baptism. But John, being a prophet and knowing who asked, at first denied him baptism, but later consented that all the prophecies might be fulfilled. For here was a man born without sin.

``Now sin is the great scourge of the age - indeed, of any age. And you here today who know his plan, and are able through your knowledge of the Law truly to appreciate moral values, can, therefore, confidently look upon yourself as a guide to those who do not know the way, and as a light to those who are groping in the dark. You can instruct those who have no spiritual wisdom: you can teach those who, spiritually speaking, are only just out of the cradle. You have a certain grasp of the basis of true knowledge. You have without doubt very great advantages. But, prepared as you are to instruct others, do you ever teach yourself anything? You preach against stealing, for example, but are you sure of your own honesty? You denounce the practice of adultery, but are you sure of your own purity? You loathe idolatry, but how honest are you towards the property of heathen temples?

``Is there any advantage then in being one of the chosen people? Yes, at least to an extent - you have only to think of one thing to begin with - it was the Jews to whom God's messages were entrusted. Some of them were undoubtedly faithless, but what of it? Can you imagine that their faithlessness could disturb the faithfulness of God? Of course not! Let us think of God as true, even if every living man be proved a liar. Remember the scripture? 'That you may be justified in your words, and may overcome when you are judged'.

``So then, if those who know the Law cannot avoid sin, what hope is there for the Emperor Claudius and his harlot who do not know the Law? And so, having sent many prophets to emphasize the Law and to instruct in the Law, he went a step further and sent Jesus, to call all sinners to repentance that in their repentance they might usher in the day of salvation.

``But what happened? In the mortal sphere, it would seem that sin overcame the power of God! This man, a man in whom even the prophet John saw no sin, was taken by the chiefs of the very people he was trying to save and was found guilty of trumped-up charges and handed over to the Romans for execution!

``And not just any execution - the demeaning and horrible crucifixion meted out to rebellious slaves! The very representative of God on earth! How could this be? How could God stand for it?

``A good question, my friends. A very good question. And the simple answer is that God did not stand for it. He allowed Jesus to be taken, it is true, and he allowed men to think that they had killed him, true, but after three days he took him and brought him forth from the tomb and showed him to his followers and many others as a sign of the resurrection which is to come at the end of time!

``Indeed, indeed there may be some here who subscribe to the party of the Sadduccees and deny that there is any resurrection in the body. I myself was raised a Pharisee and believe in the resurrection. But this is no longer an open question to be debated! God has offered an infallible proof in the risen Jesus.

``And that can only mean one thing, my friends. If the resurrection has started, we must be in the endtimes! We must now be in the days of which the prophet Jeremiah said, `And I will write my Law in their inward parts and they shall be called my people.'

``Now is the time, my friends, right now is the day of salvation! You are called on to believe in the power of God and the sacrifice of his messenger, the Messiah Jesus.

``What does God ask of you, my children? What has he ever asked of you? To love good, and to abstain from evil. To be good, to love your neighbor, to show kindness to all.

``What then has changed? Just this: through this one, immense sacrifice of a man unquestionably good; a man who came to save all of us but was himself killed; through this, I say, the rules of the game have changed overnight.

``Previously, the Law was like a giant scoreboard. You obeyed this law, you disobeyed that one. Sin and goodness were in perpetual warfare. But now God freely pours out his holy spirit on all who will receive it - accept this holy spirit and be free of sin, of temptation, of evil - and of the need to be scrupulous in your every action to make sure it accords with the Law!

``It is not the Law has changed, or been abrogated. How could that be? But rather, that by accepting the spirit of Christ - the spirit of God - you raise yourself up onto a plane where the sin can no longer tempt you and you follow the principles of the Law as unthinkingly as you currently follow the dictates of Sin!

``Now, when you receive baptism - a regular baptism, the baptism that John would offer you - it signifies a change of heart, a desire to move in a different direction. That is what we mean by baptism: washing away the collected sins of the world and making a clean slate.

``But the new baptism - the baptism of Jesus, the baptism I am offering you today - is more than that. The baptism of Jesus is not water on the outside, washing away past sins, but fire on the inside, scourging the sinful members.

``My friends, this is the time prophesied. This is the time to repent and return to God! This time it really is Now or Never! There is so little time left for second chances!

``God will always be the victor.''
